## Required script development
1. **Richer premise, immediate readable problem.** Open on an action, desire or threat that makes the viewer ask a concrete question. Do not spend the opening touring a static set.
2. **Distinct escalating attempts.** Use goal→attempt→changed consequence→different attempt. Each failure changes a prop, position, relationship, available option or urgency. No reset to the same state with cosmetic wiggles.
3. **Purposeful faster action, still readable.** Remove dead travel, repeated demonstrations and unchanged waiting. Author anticipation, decisive action, contact, consequence and a brief meaningful reaction separately. Fast does not mean every motion has the same speed.
4. **Surprise and earned payoff.** Seed important resources/props, preserve cause and effect, exploit character choices, and resolve or reverse expectations. Prefer a final character-based gag or loop over unrelated particles or arbitrary added chaos.
5. **Warm expressive characters.** Eye direction, pose and gesture should express intention or changed understanding. Preserve Moki/Peb identity and friendship; idle movement is polish, not a story event.
6. **Duration follows content.** Original source durations are not binding when they create padding. No fixed30s rule for all shorts, no fixed100s rule for longs. A long must earn its extra time through richer dramatic turns, not stretch a short. Extend a necessary read/reaction rather than cram mechanisms into an arbitrary target.
7. **No global-speed shortcut.** Re-author timing, actions and consequences; do not just accelerate an existing MP4 or resample its soundtrack. Rebuild audio to the actual revised events.
8. **CTA does not stall the ending.** Retain the required wording while choosing a legible duration and gentle supporting action. It must not conceal or compete with the payoff; a static hold is not automatically justified by a template.

## Independent creative QA before publication
- Preserve a before/after beat map, source/adaptation identities, removed waits, new consequences, and intentional readable holds.
- A fresh reviewer should be able to state the goal, why each attempt changes the problem, why the resolution works, and why the payoff happens. Review actual encoded dense sequences and selected full-resolution frames, not the prose alone.
- Prefer actual1× audiovisual review when a supported route exists. If unavailable, disclose that limitation and perform a bounded causal/visual assessment; never invent listening, continuous playback or audience approval.
- Review low-information spans and explain keep/cut choices. Event counts/density are descriptive and depend on grouping; they do not prove entertainment or retention.
- Technical passes (geometry, decode, deterministic seek, audio correlation, hashes) cannot establish comedy, pacing or audience enjoyment. A user rejection reopens creative acceptance even when technical QA passed.
- After creative edits, rerun affected physical, ownership, swept collision, camera/silhouette, audio synchronization and encoded-review gates on exact final hashes. Preserve rejected attempts and their reasons.

## Comprehension and semantic sound gate — learned from rejected EP031 preview
- Before a costly render, create6–8 storyboard frames and request an unbriefed interpretation. A reviewer who has read the intended plot is not a substitute for this test. Fix unclear goals/objects/causes first.
- Use recognizable sounds for the actual action. For a sneeze: recognizable nonverbal anticipatory breath/suppression/achoo, not generic noise sweeps. Distinguish relief sigh, material impacts and other causes; duck music so the action reads.
- Research legitimate synthesis/recording/licensed assets without purchases or credentials unless separately authorized. Keep license/provenance.
- Numerical sound metrics establish clipping, reconstruction or timing only—not semantic recognition. Do not claim the viewer will understand a sound without appropriate evidence. When no listening/semantic validation route exists, disclose and keep that gate unresolved rather than manufacture a pass.
- The user’s “incomprehensible” verdict on EP031 invalidated its positive bounded creative review. More events and shorter duration are not sufficient; clear meaning comes first.
