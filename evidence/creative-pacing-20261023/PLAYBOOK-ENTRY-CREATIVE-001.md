**Status:** diagnosed; proposed correction awaiting animatic/encoded validation. Not a proven repair.

## Evidence

## Diagnosis
Timing slots and mechanical continuity dominate over causal escalation. Repeated unchanged situations, long transit/operations and tiny expressions in wide staging consume time. Existing geometry/encode QA does not test audience understanding, suspense, surprise or comedic rhythm. Audience effect beyond the user's report remains a hypothesis.

## Proposed correction
1. Author goal→attempt→changed consequence→different attempt→earned reversal before assigning duration.
2. Every attempt inherits a cost or changed state; no full reset except deliberate comedic pattern.
3. Remove redundant travel/process; preserve anticipation/contact/meaningful reaction. Do not globally accelerate playback.
4. Design purposeful staging and contrasting motion accents, not random cuts or constant movement.
5. Seed payoff resource; reveal promptly after earned success. A long must earn more dramatic turns, not stretch a short.
6. EP030 alternative treatment targets100 story seconds, replacing water arithmetic with brake-held transfer and strengthening causal failure/cooperation. Canonical script and scheduled asset remain unchanged.

## Tests required before calling fixed
- Fresh reviewer watches timed animatic1×; states goal, stakes, distinct attempts and payoff without explanatory notes.
- Review each low-information span (>~2s short/>~3s long) with reasoned keep/cut; thresholds are review flags, not rigid laws.
- Independent before/after comparison: log removed waits, new consequences and retained readability.
- Re-render; rerun all physical/ownership/sweep/camera/audio tests after timing changes.
- Continuous encoded1× audiovisual review plus selected full-resolution inspections; record real reviewer/scope/time and candidate hashes.
- Mark not fixed until that evidence exists. Later retention data is useful but presently absent.
