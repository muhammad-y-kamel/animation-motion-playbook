## Boundaries
This is scoped saved-pixel acceptance, not normal-speed playback or listening. The illustration is qualitative, not a quantitative biochemical simulation. Reactive direct-child monitoring is not a proven parent-loss containment system. The original Nov1 six-frame proof lost during workspace reset was not recovered or reclassified; Nov2 evidence is a fresh run. Published content and canonical narration were not changed.

## Reusable lesson
Check moving trajectories against the complete annotation envelope at the actual entry/exit interval, not merely static source anchors or a few convenient witness times. Preserve technically successful but visually held outputs. Prefer a short complete rerender with existing verified QA over inventing a new patch helper when the episode is small.
