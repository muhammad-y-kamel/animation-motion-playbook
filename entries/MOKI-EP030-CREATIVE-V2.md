# EP030 creative-v2: observed failures, trials and prevention

User feedback on 2026-10-23: long episodes were very slow, event-poor and boring; short episodes also had weak ideas and slow action. The subsequent EP031 preview was rejected as incomprehensible, with inadequate recognizable action sounds. Those user observations invalidate technical-only acceptance and apply to all future MOKI & PEB work.

## CREAT-V2-001 — Unreachable pointing target
Source preview at5.5s stretched Peb's arm toward a destination before walking there. Rejected source retained. Changed pointing to a root-relative hand with gaze toward destination. Reach and visual review must include intermediate staging, not target coordinates alone.

## CREAT-V2-002 — Ladder transition discontinuity
Independent review found sudden hands at93/98s and body/foot drop at98s. Added pregrip92–93, actual floor landing and release98–98.4. Sampled boundary continuity and painted support checks pass on final frozen source, not arbitrary mesh proof.

## CREAT-V2-003 — Counterweight support geometry
Inherited track visually rose despite gravity-roll premise. First fix sloped the support but introduced deck/brace collision; failed attempts preserved. Revised tapered track, limited brace and actual rotated wheel support. Structural regression required in addition to foot checks.

## CREAT-V2-004 — Body/crate collision escaped foot tests
Peb's upper crossing at80–82s intersected delivered crate. Added explicit supported takeoff, airborne path and landing; body/crate and camera envelope tests. Mixed-source render risk addressed through per-segment source hash and final source binding.

## CREAT-V2-005 — Story logic and hidden controls
Blind chronological-frame interpretation understood teamwork and delivery, but could not confidently explain first blocked delivery, automatic cargo ram, weight release, or ladder irony. Earlier informed reviews had insufficiently challenged intent. Trials: visible red stop; goal pictogram; operated control; peg close-up; anchored shelf; motor/ram; highlighted cable. Removed a consequence-free hop. Final joke is the newly available ladder interrupting Moki's habit of taking the elaborate route DOWN, not a claim that the locked ladder was available beforehand. Independent bounded recheck found the sampled causal links clearer; continuous audience comprehension remains unproven.

## CREAT-V2-006 — Clarification prop creates collision
Permanently adding the upper ram improved causal explanation but occupied Peb's body region while he rode. Preserved trial. Mounted motor below deck; rising pusher exists only50.6–55.5 after Peb transfers away. Every story clarification must re-run collision checks; explanatory props are not exempt.

## CREAT-V2-007 — Obsolete audio verifier and cue
Recorded CC0 Foley overlay invalidated an old music+synth sum assertion. Preserved failure; new verifier reconstructs independent current mix equation including ducking/gain/fade, verifies assets, and repeats builds. Removed59s jump puff after hop removal. Numerical pass is neither listening nor semantic recognition.

## CREAT-V2-008 — Labels do not establish recognizable sound
Replaced generic synthetic action layers with licensed recorded rope/ratchet/wood/metal/effort sounds, with music ducking. Asset titles, event alignment, peak checks and successful decoding do not prove perception. Independent waveform classifier is additional imperfect evidence, not human listening; low or contradictory class scores must be reported, not relabeled as a pass.

## General prevention
1. Require readable goal, obstacle, action and consequence before polishing animation.
2. Test a fresh reviewer without supplying intended story; preserve uncertainty and conflicting readings.
3. Do not substitute more events or shorter runtime for comprehension.
4. Use source and physics checks to support, never replace, encoded visual and semantic audio review.
5. Freeze source before encode; any subsequent change invalidates affected segments and review.
6. Separate proposed fix, source-tested fix, encoded-tested fix, audience feedback and publication state.

## CREAT-V2-009 — Thumbnails missed new motor-floor penetration
The first below-deck motor avoided the character but extended below the floor when its deck reached the bottom. Exhaustive small thumbnails did not reveal it; native-resolution early frames did. Preserved the failed source and encoded output. Reduced and remounted the motor wholly between deck and floor; added 24,000 samples including painted stroke and a dedicated native encoded recheck. General lesson: exhaustive frame count is not exhaustive defect coverage. Newly introduced geometry needs native-resolution inspection at worst-case extrema, plus explicit support/floor/stroke tests.
