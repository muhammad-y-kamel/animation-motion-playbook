## Root cause and repair under way
One eased progress value, effectively `ease(t / entire_semantic_shot_duration)`, stretched a normal physical gesture across a 9–13s narration beat. Camera movement and tiny head tilt then substituted for subject acting. Endpoint-only inspection missed slow movement and missing use/contact events.

Separate the **semantic shot clock** from a **finite physical action clock**. Schedule explicit anticipation → reach → contact/grip → actual use/transfer → release/withdraw, with natural action-specific durations (roughly 0.4–1.2s for an ordinary reach, not a universal speed mandate). A hammer must visibly strike its intended surface, not only rise. Long spoken beats need multiple source-motivated actions, reactions or detail cuts—not one glacial gesture or arbitrary frantic motion. Keep narration timing and approved words unchanged.

Use an absolute-time, pure event ledger for each prop's identity, owner, location/support and transfer state. State at any timestamp must reconstruct independently; mutable state accumulated from previous rendered frames breaks segmented rendering/seeking. A bag/basil/ring cannot disappear, teleport or duplicate between shots. Retain left/right doorway geography, source-correct eyelines and genuine articulated locomotion.
