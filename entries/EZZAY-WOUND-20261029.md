## Prevention
- Keep medical negation and referent-bearing phrases together in captions; preserve words/timing rather than regenerate speech.
- Audit original codec delay/padding and actual decoded final speech before interpreting container duration differences as truncation.
- Validate the job schema before allocating the encoder.
- Separate script approval, science review, source geometry, encoded visual coverage, numerical audio checks, auditory review, optional paid QA, Library durability and externally verified publication.
